## 3. Grounds for suspicion

1. **Rapid pass-through of funds.** 86% of the inbound amount (HKD 313,535 of HKD 364,586) left the account within 20 hours, in two transfers of an identical amount. The account was used as a conduit rather than for a legitimate purpose.
2. **No economic rationale.** The customer's declared occupation and profile do not explain a HKD 364,586 inflow from JUR-B, nor two outgoing transfers of exactly HKD 156,767.58 to two different jurisdictions.
3. **Jurisdiction risk.** JUR-B and JUR-C are on the institution's higher-risk jurisdiction list. The same funds moved between two such jurisdictions in under a day.
4. **Off-cycle activity pattern.** Transfers were initiated at 22:38 and 03:38 local time through online banking — inconsistent with the customer's historical behaviour, which is low-value, domestic and branch-based.
5. **Structuring of the outflow.** The identical split of the outgoing amount (two equal transfers) is consistent with an attempt to stay below monitoring thresholds applied at the receiving institution.

## 5. Action taken

- Alert escalated from automated monitoring to Level 2 review on 2025-05-03.
- Account placed under enhanced monitoring; no further outbound transfers above HKD 50,000 without manual approval.
